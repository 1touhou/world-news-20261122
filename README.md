A one-page newspaper infographic summing up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026: COP31 climate talks in Antalya nearing a final deal after all-night negotiations; Donald Trump vowing to work with the incoming Democratic Congress on a 10% cap on credit card interest; the Pentagon curbing military cooperation with Canada; a Russian missile strike on Kryvyi Rih, President Zelenskyy's home city, killing 2 and wounding 60; the EU proposing a 3% tax on Big Tech to fund its next budget; and 24 people dead after a boat carrying mourners to a funeral capsizes in Bayelsa State, Nigeria. The image is drawn entirely in code by `main.py` (stdlib + Pillow, no network, no external fonts or images).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes `entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB) to the current directory, or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when that variable is set. Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
